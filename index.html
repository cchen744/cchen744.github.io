01About

I'm an undergraduate Data Science student at UW–Madison with a focus on geospatial analysis — the intersection of statistics, geometry, and geography. My work spans spatial statistics (Moran's I, LISA, GWR), graph-based street network analysis, satellite remote sensing, and building spatial algorithms from scratch rather than only calling library functions. I'm most interested in projects where the data has real coordinates and the output is a map someone can act on.
