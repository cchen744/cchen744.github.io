01About

Welcome to my world of maps. I'm currently a graduate student in Applied Statistics and Data Science at Cornell University. I earned a B.S. in Data Science from the University of Wisconsin–Madison, along with a certificate in Geospatial Data Science—the intersection of statistics, geometry, and geography. My work spans spatial statistics (Moran's I, LISA, and GWR), graph-based street network analysis, satellite remote sensing, and implementing spatial algorithms beyond simply relying on existing libraries. I'm most interested in projects where data has real-world coordinates and the result is a map that helps people understand, decide, and act.
